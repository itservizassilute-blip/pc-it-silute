Privacy Policy

This privacy policy defines how "PC IT Šilutė" collects, uses, and protects your personal data when you fill out the contact form on the website or apply for computer repair services.

1. Contacts

If you have any questions regarding data protection, you can contact us via:

  • Email: itservizassilute@gmail.com
  • Phone: +370 691 87006

2. What Data We Collect and Why

Data provided in the contact form (name, email address, phone number, message text) is processed to respond to customer inquiries, provide consultations, and prepare for service delivery. The legal basis for processing is actions requested by the client prior to concluding a service agreement and the provision of services.

These inquiries are delivered to us through a third-party service provider, "Formspree," which handles form submissions in accordance with the Formspree Privacy Policy.

3. Website Analytics and Cookies

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to analyze website traffic statistics. This tool does not use any cookies and does not store any data on your device that could identify you – visitor uniqueness is calculated instantaneously on the server side from the IP address and browser data, then immediately discarded. No personally identifying data is stored anywhere, which is why this tool does not require separate consent or a cookie banner.

4. Data Security and Data Recovery

During repair and data recovery, any client file content that may be accessed is handled strictly as necessary to provide the specific service. It is not published, used for other purposes, or transferred to third parties, except when necessary to perform the service and agreed upon with the client in advance.

5. Retention Periods

Inquiry data is stored for as long as necessary to process the inquiry and maintain related communication, but for no longer than 1 year, except where longer retention is required by legal acts (e.g., for accounting purposes) or warranty service.

6. Your Rights and Complaints

You have the right to access your processed data, request correction, deletion, or restriction of processing. If you believe your personal data is being processed improperly, you have the right to file a complaint with the State Data Protection Inspectorate (VDAI): vdai.lrv.lt.
